## 🕹️ Game Controls & How to Play

1. **Token Attention Cascades (Candy Crush)**:
   - Click a token, then click an adjacent token to swap.
   - Matching 3 tokens reduces **Model Loss** and increases **Compute Score**.
   - Matching 4 tokens spawns a **Flash Attention Beam (⚡)** that clears an entire row or column.
   - Matching 5 tokens spawns an **Attention Singularity (🌌)** that clears all tokens of that type.
2. **Agentic Tactical Moves (Chess)**:
   - Click on your blue agent piece (👑 Orchestrator, ⚔️ Executor, 🔮 Critic, 🛡️ Memory).
   - Valid moves highlight in glowing green. Click any highlighted cell to advance.
   - Landing on a cell harvests that token, triggers local cascades, and clears hazards!
   - Capture enemy red pieces for **+500 Compute** and instant power-ups.
3. **Mystery Blocks & Hazards (Mario)**:
   - Match next to a **`?` Block** to crack it open.
   - Beware of **Hallucination Bombs (💣)**: Defuse them with your Critic or by matching nearby before their turn timer expires!
4. **Download Your Credentials**:
   - Click **🏆 Badges & Certs** in the top navigation bar.
   - Type your name into the text box to watch the certificate update in real time.
   - Click **📄 Download Official PDF** or **🖼️ Download High-Res PNG**!
   - Click any badge card to download its high-resolution PNG shield.
